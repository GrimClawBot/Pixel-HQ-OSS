# Pixel HQ Community Code of Conduct

Pixel HQ welcomes technical discussion and contributions from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- treat others with respect and good faith;
- critique ideas, code, architecture, and evidence rather than people;
- keep technical disagreement focused on facts, tradeoffs, tests, and project requirements;
- respect security, privacy, and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- avoid knowingly submitting private, confidential, credential, or personally sensitive information;
- disclose when a contribution was materially produced with AI assistance when that context is relevant to review;
- accept that security-sensitive changes may require additional review even when the code appears functionally correct.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in Pixel HQ community spaces:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory abuse;
- sexualized or deliberately degrading comments directed at participants;
- doxxing or publishing another person's private information without permission;
- knowingly publishing credentials, secrets, private infrastructure details, or security-sensitive information that should use private disclosure;
- repeated bad-faith disruption of issues, pull requests, reviews, or community discussions;
- impersonating maintainers or falsely claiming approval, authority, security status, or project affiliation.

## Security-sensitive discussions

Do not use public issues or discussions to publish exploit details that could create immediate risk, private keys/tokens, hidden privileged-security procedures, or private infrastructure information.

Use the private vulnerability-reporting route defined by the public `SECURITY.md` once the public repository launches.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and discussions that violate this code, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors, the project, or its security boundaries.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and based on behavior rather than disagreement with a maintainer.

## Scope

This code applies to Pixel HQ project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed collaboration channels.

It does not override the project's technical/security governance or responsible-disclosure requirements.

## Reporting conduct concerns

Before public launch, conduct concerns should be sent privately to the repository owner. The final public repository should provide a suitable private contact or moderation route before accepting broad community participation.
